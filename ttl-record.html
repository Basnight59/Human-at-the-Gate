Human at the Gate / Part 04 of 07

Documentation: create your own auditable history

We do not rely on anyone else's records to prove what we did. We keep our own.

The Truth, Transparency and Legacy (TTL) record preserves, with dates:

  • every outgoing communication: recipient, content, attachments and the human who authorized it
  • every response, including terms or conditions attached to it and how we answered
  • every failure, such as a bounced email, and what was done about it
  • the dated drafts and AI sessions from which each idea emerged

Added, never edited away

Entries are added, never edited away. The chain runs:

  1. Human judgment
  2. Authorization
  3. Signed communication
  4. Transmission
  5. Timestamp
  6. Archival record
  7. Accountability

The record protects authorship

Our frameworks are independently developed syntheses originating from our own research. The dated archive shows what was developed, when, and by whom. Whether others have independently reached similar ideas is a question for comparative scholarship; it does not affect the origin of our work.
