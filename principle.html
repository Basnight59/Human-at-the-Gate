Human at the Gate / Part 01 of 07

Ethical responsibility belongs to human beings

Ethics are the responsibility of human beings, not of AI or of anything else humans create. A tool can carry values that people built into it. It cannot bear the trust.

Older than any technology

This principle is older than any technology. The Qur'an describes the Trust (amanah) offered to the heavens, the earth and the mountains, which declined to bear it; the human being took it on (33:72). Moral responsibility was placed on human beings, and it does not transfer to what they make.

The first family

The first family shows both sides of that trust.

7:12

The first refusal

“I am better than him” is the arrogance from which hierarchy and injustice grow.

5:27–32

The first murder

One of Adam's sons killing the other produced the principle that taking one life unjustly is as if one had taken all of humanity.

From the beginning, ethics meant one person's responsibility for another.

Adopted as policy

No AI output becomes an action until a named human being has reviewed it, authorized it and accepted responsibility for it.
