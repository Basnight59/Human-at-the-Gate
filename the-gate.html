Human at the Gate / Part 02 of 07

The human at the gate

The gate is the point between preparation and action. AI works on the near side of it; only the responsible human may open it.

AI may
Gate
Only the responsible human may
Research, gather and compare sources
Decide which sources are trustworthy
Draft letters, papers and submissions
Approve the final wording
Challenge claims and flag errors
Resolve disagreements between platforms
Organize records and prepare actions, such as attaching files to a draft
Authorize any send, publication or commitment
Recommend next steps
Sign, stand behind and answer for the result

In practice

  • Every email carries the human author's name and is sent only on that person's explicit instruction.
  • Every public document is reviewed by its author before release.
  • When an AI system acts in a browser or an inbox, it acts only after the human has said so, and it reports what it did.

Human Responsibility Clause

This is the clause already printed in our materials: all materials remain the exclusive responsibility of the human author and adjudicator; artificial intelligence systems function solely as analytical instrumentation.
