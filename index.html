Policy on Human-Centered AI

Human at the Gate

Sphinx Global Enterprises Corp. · Kenneth Basnight · October 24, 2026

At Sphinx Global Enterprises, artificial intelligence may research, compare, draft, challenge and recommend, but only a responsible human being may authorize, publish or act, and that human answers for the result. We call this principle the human at the gate.

Read the policy Our commitments

Summary

What this paper covers

This paper sets out the principle, the method that puts it into practice (the Sphinx Method), the documentation that makes it auditable (the TTL record), and a worked example from our October 2026 outreach. It applies to all Sphinx Global Enterprises work that uses AI, including Sphinx Analysis and the 56th Star Initiative.

The question is not whether AI can be ethical. The question is which human being answers for what the AI did.
