Human at the Gate / Part 06 of 07

In practice: the October 2026 outreach

Between 22 and 24 October 2026, the 56th Star Initiative sent its manuscript The Diaspora Debate and supporting briefs to scholars, African Union bodies, the United Nations, CARICOM and the embassies of nine African states.

The method ran as described.

01

AI prepared, the human authorized

AI systems researched contacts, drafted letters (including French and Portuguese versions), attached files and logged results. No letter was sent until the author gave an explicit instruction to send, after reviewing it.

02

Platforms checked each other

A second platform caught an overstated delivery count (20 where the correct figure was 17), and the record was corrected. Two platforms independently identified the same gap: an operational Shura Mandate not yet drafted.

03

Failures were recorded, not hidden

Bounced addresses, a domain no longer accepting mail and a recipient who had changed institutions were each logged with the action taken.

04

Terms were recorded openly

One scholar offered a review for a fee; the author declined, keeping review unpaid and open to all, and both the offer and the reply entered the record.

05

“Delivered” was not overstated

The record distinguishes a message that did not bounce from one known to have been read.

A reply within two hours

One institution, the UN Permanent Forum on People of African Descent, replied within two hours with a formal invitation to submit inputs, which is itself now part of the record.
