Commitments and limits

Sphinx Global Enterprises commits to the following.

  1. 1

    Every action taken with AI assistance has a named, responsible human author.

  2. 2

    No communication, publication or commitment is made without that person's explicit authorization.

  3. 3

    AI outputs are compared and challenged before they are trusted, and disagreements are resolved by a person, not by vote.

  4. 4

    Every authorized action is documented in the TTL record with its date, basis and outcome.

  5. 5

    Errors, failures and corrections are recorded as openly as successes.

  6. 6

    Reviews and responses from others are invited freely and recorded honestly, without payment for endorsement.

  7. 7

    Our work states its authorship plainly and does not claim more than the evidence shows.

Limits

This policy governs our own conduct. It does not certify the accuracy of any AI system, and it does not replace legal, medical or financial advice where those are needed. The cognitive-participation claim is a hypothesis awaiting study.

The policy itself is written in pencil: it will be revised as practice teaches us, and each revision will be dated.
