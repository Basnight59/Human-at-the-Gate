Human at the Gate / Part 03 of 07

The Sphinx Method

The Sphinx Method is how we use several AI platforms in parallel without surrendering judgment to any of them.

Its motto: we take real-world problems and create real-world algorithms, meaning repeatable, auditable processes, not only software.

  1. 01

    Orchestrate

    The human poses the question to one or more platforms.

  2. 02

    Compare

    Outputs are read, and often heard through audio playback, side by side.

  3. 03

    Challenge

    Each platform's claims are tested against the others and against sources.

  4. 04

    Reconcile

    The human resolves disagreements, deciding which claim holds.

  5. 05

    Synchronize

    The human judges that evidence, reasoning and conscience have converged.

  6. 06

    Authorize

    The human approves the action.

  7. 07

    Document

    The decision, its basis and its time are recorded.

  8. 08

    Act

    The authorized step is taken, and the human answers for it.

Synchronization is not consensus

Several models agreeing does not make a claim true; they can share the same error. Synchronization is the moment the human orchestrator, using reason, experience, intuition, conscience and qalb, judges that the synthesis is sound enough to act on. The most valuable moments are often disagreements, because they expose what one system missed.

Not automated orchestration

Automated pipelines

One model's output passes directly to the next with no person present.

The Sphinx Method

A person reads, hears and judges at every handoff, and interacts by voice as well as text.
